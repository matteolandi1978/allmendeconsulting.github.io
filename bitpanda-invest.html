How this allocation is calculated

Every time you compute a guide, the page pulls fresh public price data from CoinGecko for a fixed universe of 16 major assets listed on Bitpanda (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOT, LINK, AVAX, LTC, BCH, ATOM, UNI, XLM, TRX, NEAR, DOGE). Nothing is guessed or hard-coded — it's recomputed from that day's numbers.

  • Core (BTC + ETH): always funded first, split 60% BTC / 40% ETH of the core budget. This is a fixed dollar-cost-averaging foundation regardless of short-term signals.
  • Core vs satellite split depends on risk profile: Conservative 70/30, Balanced 50/50, Growth 30/70.
  • Satellite scoring for the other 14 assets combines four signals computed from 90 days of daily prices:
    • Trend — is price above its 20-day and 50-day moving average?
    • Momentum — 7-day and 30-day price change.
    • RSI(14) — rewards a healthy 45–65 range, penalizes overbought (>75) and weak (<30) readings.
    • Volatility — 30-day return volatility is subtracted as a penalty, so calmer uptrends outrank erratic spikes.
  • Only assets with a net-positive composite score are eligible. They're weighted by score (softmax), capped per-asset (10–20% depending on risk profile) and redistributed, so no single altcoin can dominate the satellite sleeve.
  • If fewer assets qualify than the profile's max position count, the unused budget simply isn't force-allocated — you'll see fewer rows that week.

This is one defensible systematic approach among many — it is deliberately simple and auditable rather than optimized for backtested returns. Treat it as a structured starting point for your own research, not a signal to follow blindly.
